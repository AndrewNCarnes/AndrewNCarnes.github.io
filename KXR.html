IREC Odysseus — Aerostructures Lead

Knights Experimental Rocketry • International Rocket Engineering Competition 2026

Overview

I lead aerostructures design and manufacturing for UCF's Knights Experimental Rocketry "Odysseus" rocket, our entry in the International Rocket Engineering Competition (IREC) 2026. My work spans composite layup, fiberglass and wet-layup fabrication, jig design, structural analysis, and integration planning to ensure airframe integrity from launch through recovery.

Responsibilities & Contributions

  • Directing composite layup and manufacturing for the International Rocket Engineering Competition 2026 (IREC).
  • Coordinating cross-team hardware integration between propulsion, recovery, controls, and payload systems.
  • Collaborating with UCF SHPE to design and manufacture a fiberglass nosecone for the "Sunflower" payload.
  • Developed Manufacturing Process Plans (MPPs) for carbon fiber and fiberglass wet-layup processes toward the creation of the nosecone, body tubes, and other structures — cutting wet-layup time by over 36% and reducing process errors.

Skills Applied

Composite layup, fiberglass and wet-layup fabrication, jig design, structural analysis, Manufacturing Process Plan (MPP) development, and cross-functional integration planning.
